EGM Phase E4: Specification v4.0 (Progressive UI & Unified Architecture)

CountdownMaker: 30-Second Cinematic Video Generator

Progressive Step-by-Step UI Disclosure, Real-Time Generation Telemetry & Unified Single-Port Architecture

1. Executive Summary & Objective

CountdownMaker automates the creation of 30-second cinematic countdown videos (counting down sequentially from 10 to 1) synchronized to a fixed 30-second soundtrack located at public/countdown/countdown_track.mp3.

v4.0 Key UI & Architectural Upgrades:
  • Progressive Step-by-Step UI Disclosure: The frontend only displays the phase currently required by the user, progressively unlocking subsequent stages upon successful completion:
    1. Stage 1: Theme & Brand Definition (Active upon start)
    2. Stage 2: 10 Diegetic Shots Review & Refinement (Revealed only after prompts and image generation start, with a live generation progress banner)
    3. Stage 3: Veo 3 Video Synthesis (Revealed once images are generated and reviewed)
    4. Stage 4: Audio Waveform Timeline & Sub-Second Alignment (Revealed once videos are ready)
    5. Stage 5: Final Master 30s Export (Revealed for master concat assembly)
  • Visual Generation Feedback & Telemetry Banner: Animated generation status banner showing active slot count, progress bar ($0\% \to 100\%$), and per-card pulsating glow to provide unambiguous visual cues that generation is running.
  • Unified Single-Server Architecture: The Express backend builds and serves the frontend client and API endpoints on a single port (http://localhost:3000 or http://localhost:5173) eliminating proxy connection failures.

3. Acceptance Criteria (v4.0)

  1. Initial view presents only Stage 1 (Theme & Brand form) with a clean, focused interface.
  2. Clicking "Synthesize" immediately reveals Stage 2 with an animated generation banner and progress indicator.
  3. Stage 3, 4, and 5 unlock sequentially as dependencies complete.
  4. Unified single-port server ensures zero proxy errors.
