EGM Phase E6 Specification

Magic Countdown Generator (Specification v6.0)

30-Second Cinematic Countdown Generator with Gemini Nano Banana (gemini-2.5-flash-image) and Veo 3 Video Synthesis

1. Architecture & Model Integration

The Magic Countdown Generator synthesizes a cohesive 30.0-second broadcast countdown video comprising 10 distinct, photorealistic 16:9 shots counting down from 10 down to 1 with diegetically embedded physical numbers.

Model: gemini-2.5-flash-image

Gemini Nano Banana

Direct multimodal image synthesis producing photorealistic 1024x1024 / 16:9 PNGs with diegetically integrated physical typography.

Model: Google Veo 3

Veo 3 Video Generation

4.0-second 60fps dynamic camera push-in zooms generated without audio (-an, -preset ultrafast) for rapid rendering, with the 30.0s master soundtrack overlaid exclusively during final master compilation.

2. Viewport & Layout Architecture

To maximize visual clarity across laptop screens and high-resolution displays:

  • Full-Width Stacked Feed: Shot cards are stacked vertically (flex flex-col gap-8 w-full max-w-5xl mx-auto) utilizing the full horizontal resolution rather than constrained multi-column grids.
  • Side-by-Side Video & Image View (30% / 70%): Once a Veo 3 video is synthesized, the card dynamically transitions to a side-by-side view with the input frame on the left (30% width) and the high-definition Veo 3 video player on the right (70% width) for immediate visual inspection.
  • 2-Worker Bounded Parallel Queue: Both image and Veo 3 video generation pipelines use a bounded concurrency worker pool (exactly 2 concurrent workers) to maximize synthesis throughput while preventing 429 quota exhaustion and network congestion.
  • React Portal Modal System: All dialogs (Log Analysis, Test Gemini API, Model Selector) are mounted via createPortal(..., document.body) at z-[99999] to prevent clipping or occlusion behind header and main containers.
  • Clean Brand Setup: Step 1 cleanly presents Customer Brand & Aesthetic Direction with a one-click action: "Create Veo 3 Input Images".

3. Real-Time Log Analysis & Observability

The header includes a live Log Analysis modal connected to GET /api/logs:

  • Captures real-time timestamps, log levels (INFO, SUCCESS, WARN, ERROR), and categories (GEMINI_AI, ADC_AUTH, FFMPEG, SYSTEM).
  • Displays model execution latency, payload sizes (e.g. 1.66 MB PNG), and HTTP response codes.

4. API Contract Summary

GET  /api/logs                     - Returns in-memory ring buffer of server & AI events
POST /api/generate-diegetic-prompts - Synthesizes 10 prompt concepts with gemini-2.5-flash
POST /api/generate-image           - Generates 16:9 AI image with gemini-2.5-flash-image
POST /api/test-gemini-api          - Diagnostic single-image synthesis test endpoint
POST /api/refine-image             - Dual-image reference refinement
POST /api/generate-video           - 4.0s Veo 3 video compilation
POST /api/process-temporal-video   - FFmpeg speed/trim alignment
POST /api/export-master            - Concat 10 slots + 30.0s master soundtrack
