EGM Phase E8 Specification • Google Cloud Run Production

Magic Countdown Generator

30-Second Cinematic Countdown Generator powered by Gemini Nano Banana (gemini-2.5-flash-image) & Veo 3 Video Synthesis.

1. Corporate Google Single Sign-On & Dual-Domain Security

The application enforces strict cryptographic domain gating against Google's public token servers:

  • Authorized Whitelist: Strictly restricted to @cloudspace.goog and @google.com corporate accounts.
  • Unauthorized Domain Rejection: Any personal accounts (e.g. @gmail.com) or unauthorized external domains receive immediate HTTP 403 Forbidden with access blocked.
  • User Profile Header: The top-right header displays the active logged-in user profile avatar, name, verified corporate email, and a quick Sign out button.

2. Coordinated Image Framing & Dynamic Video Reveal Architecture

To maximize cinematic suspense and storytelling, image and video generation follow a coordinated reveal strategy:

📸 1. Starting Image (Reveal Framing)

In most starting images (7–8 of 10), the diegetic number does not jump out immediately in the spectator's eyes. The composition frames the machinery, atmospheric depth, and foreground occluding structures (e.g. guide vanes, carbon fiber shrouds, pipes, atmospheric steam/shadows), specifically planning for the hidden numeral to be discovered later.

🎥 2. Veo 3 Video Motion (Dynamic Reveal)

Each shot is paired with a continuous 4-second camera motion prompt (e.g. camera push-in past foreground vanes, rack focus from vapor, dynamic lighting sweep, or articulating limb) that organically brings the physically embedded diegetic numeral into sharp, crystal-clear focus during playback.

3. 5-Stage Human-in-the-Loop Workflow

  • 1Brand & Style: Customer brand definition and aesthetic direction (e.g. Porsche Motorsport).
  • 2Paired Prompt Review: Review and customize both the Starting Image Composition and the Veo 3 Motion Reveal Prompt side-by-side with single-click Gemini re-creation.
  • 3Diegetic Images: 2-Worker bounded queue synthesis with Gemini Nano Banana (gemini-2.5-flash-image).
  • 4Single-Click Accept & Video Render: Clicking "Accept Shot" immediately triggers the 4.0s Veo 3 video compilation and splits the card into 30% Starting Frame / 70% Veo 3 Reveal Video.
  • 5Master Export: Temporal waveform audio sync and 30.0s master countdown video export with native audio muxing.
