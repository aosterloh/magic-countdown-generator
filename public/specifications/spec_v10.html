2. 🚨 Core Prompt Directives for Google Veo 3.1

1. Single Unbroken One-Shot Take

Every 4-second clip is filmed in one single physical location with one continuous, fluid camera motion (dolly push-in, tracking arc, or optical zoom). Hard cuts, scene transitions, and multi-scene montages are strictly prohibited.

2. Environmental Diegesis (No Floating Text)

The countdown numeral must physically exist as an organic object in the environment (stenciled on wood, glowing on digital displays, engraved in metal, drawn on glass). No post-production floating text overlays.

3. Positive-Only Action (0.0s–2.5s)

Zero negative text prompts (no "without numbers" or "no text"). The first 2.5 seconds are described purely through positive atmospheric action, human expressions, and camera motion.

4. Centered Macro Reveal (2.5s–4.0s)

In the final 1.5 seconds, the camera locks onto the physical numeral centered in the frame in razor-sharp focus. Must conclude with: "Essential requirement: the physical numeral '[N]' must be clearly visible, centered, and unmistakably rendered in frame."
