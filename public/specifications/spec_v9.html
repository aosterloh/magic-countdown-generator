EGM Phase E11 Production Specification Version 9.0

Magic Countdown Generator

Universal Domain-Aware Directing, Zero-Hallucination Brand Safety Framing, and Multi-User Cloud Storage Persistence.

1. Executive Summary & Core Purpose

Magic Countdown Generator is an autonomous AI directing suite that creates broadcast-ready 30-second cinematic brand countdown videos (10 down to 1). The system leverages Google's flagship generative multimodal models (Gemini 2.5 Flash, Gemini Flash Image / Nano Banana, and Google Veo 3.1) alongside Google Cloud Storage (GCS) and FFmpeg to generate diegetically grounded, photorealistic video assets.

✨ EGM v9.0 Core Advancements:
  • Universal Domain Adaptation: Intelligent prompt engine infers customer industry (e.g. Sports & Padel camp vacations, Luxury Hospitality, Aviation, Automotive racing, Gastronomy) and dynamically matches the lighting, palette, and environment described in user visual ideas.
  • Zero-Hallucination Brand Safety Framing: Explicitly forbids rendering corporate logos, wordmarks, or text on physical objects, using Wide Distant establishing shots or Macro Close-ups to eliminate AI logo hallucinations.
  • Multi-User Projects Popup Modal: Search, view, open, and permanently delete past countdown projects saved in Google Cloud Storage.
  • Lossless Audio Muting: Stream-copy audio stripping (-an -c:v copy) on all Veo 3.1 clips for pristine Stage 5 soundtrack synchronization.

2. Zero-Hallucination Brand Safety & Camera Framing Architecture

Generative image and video models often hallucinate distorted typography, misspelled company names, and inaccurate logos when attempting to paint brand wordmarks on objects. EGM v9.0 enforces a strict dual-framing director's rule across all 10 scenes:

Framing Strategy A

Wide Distant Establishing Shot

The branded vehicle, facility, or environment is positioned at a wide, atmospheric distance (e.g. aircraft on misty runway, stadium arena from distant blimp angle, luxury resort from coastline). At this scale, fine typography and logos are naturally unreadable, preserving visual authenticity with zero AI text artifacts.

Framing Strategy B

Ultra Close-Up / Macro Shot

The camera focuses with extreme proximity and shallow depth-of-field (bokeh) on physical materials, equipment textures, dials, and mechanical components (e.g. racket grip tape, cockpit dial, leather stitching, tennis ball felt, titanium rotor). No corporate logos appear in the tight frame.

3. The 5-Stage Directing & Synthesis Pipeline

Stage Name Underlying Engine Key Deliverables & Behavior
1 Brand & Style Direction Client UI & Domain Analyzer Captures Customer/Brand Name, Setting/Visual Ideas, and Style Modifiers. Infers domain mood.
2 Diegetic Prompt Synthesis Gemini 2.5 Flash (API Key / ADC) Generates 10 coordinated pairs (16:9 Image Prompt + 4.0s Veo Motion Prompt) embedding numerals 10 down to 1.
3 Diegetic Starting Frames Gemini 2.5 Flash Image / Nano Banana Generates 10 starting frames via 2 parallel workers. Includes dual-image reference refinement and rollback.
4 Veo 3.1 Video Synthesis Google Veo 3.1 (Fast 720p / Full 4K) Synthesizes 4.0s @ 60fps cinematic camera motion revealing the numeral. Audio instantly stripped (-an).
5 Master Concat & Audio Sync FFmpeg Concat & EBU R128 Normalizer Sequences all 10 clips with 1.0s crossfades against a 30.0s stereo soundtrack into master_countdown.mp4.

Projects are stored persistently in Google Cloud Storage (gs://magic-countdown-generator-aosterloh-cs-muc) under a clean customer-centric directory structure:

└── jobs/
    ├── Adidas-8PF4P/
    │   ├── state.json                 <-- Full metadata, prompt cards, stage progress
    │   ├── images/shot_10_v1.png      <-- 16:9 Starting frame PNGs
    │   ├── videos/slot_10_veo.mp4     <-- Muted Veo 3.1 720p/4K MP4 clips
    │   └── master/master_30s.mp4      <-- Exported 30.0s master broadcast video
    └── Weebora_Padel-3K9LP/
        └── state.json
  • Customer-Centric Naming: Projects are named strictly by Customer/Brand Name (e.g. Adidas, Weebora, Lufthansa Group). Updating the brand field syncs the project title in real time.
  • Projects Popup Modal ([ 📁 Projects ]): Header button opens an interactive modal displaying all saved projects with stage badges (🌟 Master Ready, 🎬 Veo Videos (X/10)), timestamps, one-click Open Project, and permanent Delete Project with confirmation.
  • Continuous Live Auto-Save: Project modifications debounce and sync to GCS with live [ ☁️ Saved ] status confirmation.
  • Clean Startup: Launching the app initializes a clean project in Stage 1 without forcing past session restoration.
