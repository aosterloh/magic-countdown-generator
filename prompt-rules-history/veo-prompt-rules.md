# Universal Veo 3.1 Prompt Generation Rules & Directorial Guidelines

This document defines the prompt engineering standards and directorial blueprint for generating 10-shot countdown video prompts for Google Veo 3.1. These rules are completely **industry-agnostic**—they apply equally to any brand or sector (e.g., fashion, aviation, food & beverage, technology, sports, automotive, retail, finance, entertainment, healthcare).

---

## 1. 🎬 Universal 4-Pillar 360° Brand Narrative Arc (Zero Monotony)

Every countdown sequence (counting down from 10 to 1) must take the viewer on a complete, dynamic journey across 4 diverse narrative pillars. Shots must never feel repetitive or confined to a single room or setting.

### Pillar 1: Genesis, Creation & Craftsmanship (Shots #10, #8, #1)
- **Concept:** Where the core magic happens. Focus on origin, raw ingredients, precision crafting, R&D design, artisanal making, or high-tech assembly tailored to the customer's specific industry.
- **Shot #10 (The Genesis Moment):** The foundational beginning—design sketches, material preparation, master workshop, kitchen prep, or initial concept development.
- **Shot #8 (The Craft & Creation Process):** Active creation in motion—artisan assembly, precision machining, dynamic cooking, textile tailoring, or specialized craftsmanship.
- **Shot #1 (The Hero Climax):** The flagship master product, iconic brand emblem, or grand centerpiece presented in majestic, cinematic lighting.

### Pillar 2: Workplace Culture, People & Collaboration (Shots #9, #7)
- **Concept:** The human heartbeat of the brand. Focus on authentic team members and company atmosphere.
- **Shot #9 (Campus Life, Coffee & Lunch Moments):** Warm, inviting social space (e.g., sunlit architectural cafeteria, espresso bar, garden courtyard, or team lounge). Show identifiable colleagues smiling, laughing, and conversing naturally over a drink or snack, with soft-focus coworkers in the background.
- **Shot #7 (Creative Brainstorm & Workshop Hub):** Interactive teamwork in action—team members collaborating around a transparent glass whiteboard, design table, moodboard, or open studio workspace.

### Pillar 3: Real-World In-Use & Customer Delight (Shots #5, #4, #3)
- **Concept:** How the product or service enriches real human lives out in the world.
- **Shot #5 (High-Performance / Dynamic In-Use):** The product or service operating in an exciting, high-energy, or scenic real-world environment.
- **Shot #4 (Everyday Customer Human Connection):** An authentic individual or happy customer directly interacting with the product or service with an organic, subtle smile.
- **Shot #3 (Scale & Community Impact):** Wider real-world footprint—flagship customer destination, community gathering, sustainable infrastructure, or modern service environment.

### Pillar 4: Operations, Scale & Global Logistics (Shots #6, #2)
- **Concept:** Behind-the-scenes movement, distribution, and coordination.
- **Shot #6 (The Beat Drop / Global Distribution):** High-energy logistics, packaging, dispatch fleet, delivery transit, or bustling distribution terminal at golden hour.
- **Shot #2 (Operations Command & Control):** Modern coordination hub, flight deck, scheduling center, or mission control bridge overlooking a vibrant skyline at twilight.

## 2. 🔢 Dynamic Diegetic Number Reveals (Last 1–2 Seconds: 2.5s–4.0s)

The physical numeral `'[N]'` must appear naturally within the scene environment (diegetic) and be unveiled dynamically during seconds **2.5s to 4.0s** using a variety of cinematic techniques:

### Diverse Reveal Mechanisms to Alternate Between Shots:
1. **Cinematic Camera Push-In / Macro Lock:**
   The camera executes a rapid push-in, low-angle swoop, or macro zoom, locking directly onto the high-contrast physical numeral centered in the frame.
2. **Foreground Clearing / Natural Unmasking:**
   A foreground person, moving door, passing object, coffee cup, tray, package, or glass slide moves cleanly out of frame, revealing the centered numeral directly behind it.
3. **Human / Mechanical Finishing Action:**
   A person, tool, or mechanism performs a finishing touch (e.g., drawing on glass with a bright marker, setting down an embossed item, stamping a surface, or flipping a physical dial) that brings the number into view.
4. **Light & Contrast Activation:**
   A focused spotlight turns on, an illuminated digital display snaps to life, a shadow passes, or a dramatic rack focus shifts attention onto the high-contrast numeral.

## 3. 🚨 6 Mandatory Directives for Veo 3.1 Prompts

1. **POSITIVE-ONLY SCENE DESCRIPTIONS (0.0s–2.5s):**
   - NEVER use negative phrasing (e.g., "no numbers", "without text", "strictly zero digits"). Describe the first 2.5 seconds entirely through positive physical action, environment, and natural lighting.
2. **SINGLE ISOLATED TEXT TARGET ('[N]' ONLY):**
   - The ONLY text that should appear in single quotes anywhere in the prompt is the exact single countdown numeral `'[N]'`.
   - NEVER include labels, prefixes, or step titles (e.g., NEVER write `'STEP 10'`, `'STAGE 9'`, `'NO. 8'`). Only write `'[N]'`.
3. **HIGH-CONTRAST PHYSICAL PRESENCE:**
   - The numeral must feel physically tangible and contrast boldly with its background (e.g., polished metal on matte dark wood, bright neon marker on clear architectural glass, illuminated crisp digits on a sleek panel, high-contrast stenciled paint on composite material).
4. **CENTERED MACRO LOCK IN FINAL SECOND:**
   - In the final 1.5 seconds (2.5s–4.0s), the camera or action must lock the numeral `'[N]'` directly into the center of the frame in razor-sharp focus.
5. **MANDATORY NUMERAL VISIBILITY CLAUSE (ESSENTIAL):**
   - Every video prompt MUST explicitly end its reveal section with:
     > `"Essential requirement: the physical numeral '[N]' must be clearly visible, centered, and unmistakably rendered in frame."`
6. **CINEMATOGRAPHY SPECIFICATIONS:**
   - Always conclude with: `Cinematography: 35mm anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, volumetric rim lighting, natural skin tones, photorealistic textures, 60fps.`

## 4. 📝 Strict 3-Part Prompt Blueprint Format

```text
[0.0s-2.5s]: Dynamic wide cinematic camera tracking shot establishing [unique scene setting] for [Customer/Brand], focusing solely on [positive human action, craft, or ambient environment]. [2.5s-4.0s]: [Cinematic reveal action: camera rapidly zooms/locks in OR foreground obstacle moves cleanly away], revealing the bold high-contrast physical numeral '[N]' [tactile material and texture] occupying the center of the frame in razor-sharp focus during the final second. Essential requirement: the physical numeral '[N]' must be clearly visible, centered, and unmistakably rendered in frame. Cinematography: 35mm anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, volumetric rim lighting, natural skin tones, photorealistic textures, 60fps.
```
